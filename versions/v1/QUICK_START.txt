🔧 TROUBLESHOOTING

Problem: "No training data found"
Solution: Run data_collector_advanced.py first

Problem: "Low test accuracy (<80%)"
Solution: 
  1. Check analyze_dataset.py for sample counts
  2. Collect more data (aim for 150+ train per sign)
  3. Check confusion matrix for problematic pairs
  4. Re-collect data with better lighting

Problem: "Hand not detected"
Solution:
  1. Move hand closer to camera
  2. Ensure good lighting
  3. Keep hand fully visible (no cropping)

Problem: "Wrong predictions in real-time"
Solution:
  1. Hold sign steady for 2 seconds
  2. Wait for green border (high confidence)
  3. Check if sign is in vocabulary
  4. Retrain if accuracy is low
